## 4. Data Sharing

We share data with:
- **Healthcare providers** — treating clinicians with verified access only
- **Cloud infrastructure** — AWS (encrypted at rest and in transit)
- **Analytics vendors** — pseudonymised/anonymised datasets only

All third parties are bound by Data Processing Agreements. **We do not sell patient data.**

---

## 5. Data Security

| Safeguard | Implementation |
|-----------|---------------|
| Encryption at rest | AES-256-GCM per-patient keys |
| Encryption in transit | TLS 1.3 |
| Access controls | RBAC with mandatory MFA |
| Audit logging | Tamper-evident logs retained 7 years |
| Pseudonymisation | Applied before all AI processing |
| Penetration testing | Quarterly by independent auditors |

---

## 6. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Active patient data | Duration of Care Predict service use |
| Inactive accounts | Deleted after 90 days of inactivity |
| Audit logs | 7 years — regulatory requirement |
| Anonymised research data | Indefinite — cannot be re-identified |

Deletion follows NIST SP 800-88 secure erasure standards.

## 7. Your Rights

### GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Residents)
- **Access** — request a copy of your data
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — request deletion (responded within 30 days)
- **Portability** — receive data in FHIR or CSV format
- **Object** — object to automated decision-making
- **Withdraw Consent** — immediate effect via privacy dashboard

### HIPAA Rights (US Residents)
- Access and amendment of Protected Health Information
- Accounting of disclosures — see who accessed your records
- Request restrictions on certain uses

### CCPA Rights (California Residents)
- Right to know what data is collected and how it is used
- Right to delete personal information (within 45 days)
- Right to opt-out of data sharing (we do not sell data)

---

## 8. Automated Decision-Making

Care Predict uses AI to generate health predictions. You have the right to:
- Request human clinical review of any AI prediction
- Receive an explanation of factors influencing the prediction
- Challenge decisions that significantly affect you

---

## 9. Cross-Border Transfers

EU patient data is stored exclusively in AWS Frankfurt. US patient data in AWS US-East. Any cross-border transfers use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.
