## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.2 Personal Information

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption:** AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
- **Audit Logging:** Comprehensive logging with tamper-evident verification
- **Pseudonymization:** Patient identifiers replaced before AI processing
- **Penetration Testing:** Quarterly security assessments by independent auditors

### 5.2 Organizational Safeguards
- Privacy by design integration in development lifecycle
- Regular staff privacy and security training
- Incident response plan with 72-hour breach notification (GDPR)
- Annual privacy audits by external assessors

---

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods
- **Active Patient Data:** Retained while you use Care Predict services
- **Inactive Accounts:** Automatically deleted after 90 days of inactivity
- **Audit Logs:** Retained for 7 years per regulatory requirements
- **Anonymized Research Data:** May be retained indefinitely (cannot be linked back to you)

### 6.2 Secure Deletion
Data deletion follows NIST SP 800-88 standards for media sanitization, ensuring complete and irrecoverable removal.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Residents)
- **Right of Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to automated decision-making
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time

### 7.2 HIPAA Rights (US Residents)
- **Right to Access:** Obtain copies of your Protected Health Information
- **Right to Amendment:** Request corrections to your health records
- **Right to Accounting of Disclosures:** See who accessed your information
- **Right to Request Restrictions:** Limit certain uses and disclosures

### 7.3 CCPA Rights (California Residents)
- **Right to Know:** What personal information we collect and how we use it
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt-out of the "sale" of personal information (we do not sell data)

### 7.4 Exercising Your Rights
Contact our Data Protection Officer at [email] or use the self-service privacy dashboard. We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).
